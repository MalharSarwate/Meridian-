Routing assumptions & source

Distance-only walking model. Nodes connect only when shared in OpenStreetMap. Vehicle one-way tags are ignored for walking; explicit foot one-way tags are respected. Ways marked no/private for walking, or restricted access without foot permission, are excluded. Motorways and construction ways are excluded.

Turn restrictions, crossing safety, barrier types, opening hours, conditional access, stairs accessibility and live conditions are not fully modeled. Endpoints snap to a graph node within 120 m; the gap is not part of the route. Detours are limited to the dashed study area and may be shorter outside it. This is an exploratory calculation, not navigation advice or a traffic forecast.

Where does this data come from?

OpenStreetMap contributors, delivered as vector tiles by OpenFreeMap. Coverage and freshness vary. Building heights may be estimated or defaulted by the tile provider. These are extruded footprints, not photographic building models.

This real map does not use the demo's invented buildings, reports or commute scores. It is not a surveyed engineering drawing.
